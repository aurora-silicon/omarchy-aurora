Omarchy Aurora preserves the ALARM alarm-btrfs-v1 contract:
ESP, ext4 Boot, Btrfs Root with root/home subvolumes.
aurora-root owns in-place encryption and per-device firmware admission.
aurora-firstboot owns the first account and owner-key replacement.
Aurora's GRUB and update-aura deploy the shared kernel and Stage 2.
The desktop never installs omarchy-mac-boot's competing firstboot/Limine units.
